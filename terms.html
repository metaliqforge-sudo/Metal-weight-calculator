2. Theoretical Engineering Scope & Mandatory Physical Verification

MetaliqForge provides mathematical approximations based on theoretical geometric formulas, standardized metal alloy densities, and published ISO/DIN/ASME specs. Because hot-rolled steel, cold-drawn metals, and forged fasteners exhibit physical manufacturing rolling tolerances (±2% to ±10%), galvanization coatings, and weld seams, all output weights are strictly theoretical.

For structural bridge fabrication, aerospace engineering, crane rigging safety, or freight customs declarations, physical certified scale weighing and Mill Test Certificates (MTR) are legally and structurally required.
