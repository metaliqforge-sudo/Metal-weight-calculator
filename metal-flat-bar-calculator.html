How This Flat Bar Calculator Works

This calculator determines the theoretical weight of solid rectangular bars and strips using standard solid geometry and material density factors. The calculation is executed through two primary steps:

1. Cross-Sectional Area

Area is computed by multiplying nominal width by nominal thickness:

Area (mm²) = Width (W) × Thickness (T)

2. Mass & Unit Conversion

Multiply cross-sectional area by length and material volumetric density:

Weight (kg) = [Area (mm²) × Length (mm) × Density (g/cm³)] / 1,000,000

When imperial units are selected, dimensions are normalized internally to millimeters to retain mathematical precision and subsequently converted back into pounds (lbs) using the universal factor of 1 kg = 2.20462 lbs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is rectangular flat bar weight calculated?

Formula: Weight (kg) = Width (mm) × Thickness (mm) × Length (mm) × Density (g/cm³) / 1,000,000.

What is the difference between a plate strip and flat bar?

Hot-rolled flat bars have defined rolled square or slightly radiused edges, whereas sheared plate strips have sheared mechanical edges.
