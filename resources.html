2. Weight Calculation Formulas & Analytical Geometry

How theoretical weight is derived from geometric standard models.

Shank Thread Model

Pitch Cylinder Effective Diameter (d₂)

Rather than calculating a solid cylinder using nominal diameter (d), a fully threaded bolt possesses alternating 60° V-grooves and thread ridges. According to thread geometry, the effective diameter where material volume balances thread void is:

d₂ = d - (0.649519 × P)

Where d is nominal diameter in mm, and P is the thread pitch in mm.

Hex Head Geometry

Regular Hexagonal Prism Head Volume

The volume of a regular hexagon across flats (s) and head height (k), adjusted for standard top chamfering (approx 5% volume reduction):

Volume = (√3 / 2) × s² × k × 0.95

Where s is width across flats (AF) and k is standard nominal head height.

Total Assembly Mass Total Mass = (Head Vol + Shank Vol + Nut Vol) × Material Density
