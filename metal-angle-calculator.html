How This Calculator Works

To calculate the true cross-sectional area of an L-profile without double-counting the corner junction, the two rectangular leg areas are combined and the overlapping square corner (T × T) is deducted:

Step 1 (Cross-Section Area): Area = (Leg A + Leg B - T) × T mm²
Step 2 (Volume cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Step 3 (Single Piece Weight kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
Step 4 (Total Batch Weight): Single Piece Weight × Quantity
Standard Steel Metric Quick Formula: Weight (kg/m) ≈ (Leg A + Leg B - T) × T × 0.00785

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between equal and unequal angle bars?

Equal angles have identical leg lengths (Leg A = Leg B, e.g., 50x50x5 mm). Unequal angles have different leg dimensions (e.g., 75x50x6 mm) for directional load efficiency.

Does this calculation account for root fillets?

Hot-rolled angles feature an internal corner fillet radius and toe tapers. This calculator uses sharp-edge theoretical formulas, accurate within ±1% to 2% of actual rolling mill weight.

How do I calculate per-meter or per-foot weight?

Simply enter 1000 mm (1 meter) in Metric mode, or 12 inches (1 foot) in Imperial mode to get the immediate linear weight specification.
