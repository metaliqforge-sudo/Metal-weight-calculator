Metal Angle (L-Bar) Calculator

Profile Definition:

Leg A: Length of first leg / vertical flange.

Leg B: Length of second leg / base flange (equal or unequal).

Thickness (T): Wall thickness of the angle flange.

How This Calculator Works

To determine cross-sectional area without double-counting the corner overlap, the two leg rectangles are summed and the overlapping corner (T × T) is subtracted:

Cross-section Area: Area = [ (Leg A + Leg B - T) × T ] mm²
Volume (cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Weight (kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
