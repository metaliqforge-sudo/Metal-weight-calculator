How This Calculator Works

To calculate theoretical plate and sheet weight, the overall rectangular cubic volume is determined from length, width, and thickness, and multiplied by the material density:

Step 1 (Volume cm³): (Length mm × Width mm × Thickness mm) ÷ 1,000
Step 2 (Single Weight kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
Direct Formula (kg): (Length × Width × Thickness × Density) ÷ 1,000,000
Total Order Weight: Single Piece Weight × Quantity
Quick Steel Plate Reference: 1 m² of 1 mm thick mild steel weighs exactly 7.85 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the distinction between metal sheet and plate?

Generally in industrial standards, flat metal with thickness below 3 mm (1/8") is classified as "sheet metal", while flat metal 3 mm and above is classified as "plate".

Does plate surface finish affect theoretical weight?

Checker/diamond tread floor plates add approximately 1.5 to 2.5 kg/m² due to the raised patterns. Plain hot rolled or cold rolled plates match the theoretical calculations within ±1-2%.

Can this calculate weights of strips and flat bars?

Yes! Enter the flat bar width as "Width", thickness as "Thickness", and cut length as "Length" for instant accurate flat bar weight.
