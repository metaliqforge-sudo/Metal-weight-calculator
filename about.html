About MetaliqForge

Empowering Engineering & Manufacturing with Precision

MetaliqForge is a dedicated engineering calculation platform designed to eliminate guesswork in industrial fastener weight estimation.

Our Mission

In manufacturing, fabrication, structural assembly, and procurement, determining precise fastener weights is critical for material logistics, structural load analysis, shipping weight estimation, and exact costing. Standard charts often lack versatility or fail to factor in thread voids accurately.

MetaliqForge was built to provide a clean, free, and mathematically rigorous solution for engineers, draftsmen, hardware stockists, and students worldwide.

Mathematical Precision & Standards

Unlike simplistic calculators that approximate bolts as simple smooth solid rods, our algorithms model 60° triangular V-threads using analytical geometry:

Effective Pitch Diameter

d₂ = d - 0.6495 × Pitch

This correctly accounts for thread crests and thread root voids across full shank lengths.

Head & Drive Recess Modeling
