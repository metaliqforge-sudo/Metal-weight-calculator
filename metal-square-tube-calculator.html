How This Calculator Works

The cross-sectional area of a hollow box section is computed by subtracting the inner rectangular cavity from the outer boundary:

Step 1 (Cross-Section Area): Area = (W × H) - (W - 2×WT) × (H - 2×WT) mm²
Alternative Direct Formula: Area = 2 × WT × (W + H - 2×WT) mm²
Step 2 (Volume cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Step 3 (Weight kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
