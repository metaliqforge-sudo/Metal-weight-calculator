How This Calculator Works

The structural C-channel sectional area is computed by combining the central web section with the two horizontal flanges without double-counting the intersections:

Step 1 (Cross-Section Area): Area = (2 × B × Tf) + (H - 2 × Tf) × Tw mm²
Step 2 (Volume cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Step 3 (Single Weight kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
Step 4 (Total Batch Weight): Single Piece Weight × Quantity

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between ISMC and standard C-Channel?

ISMC (Indian Standard Medium Weight Channel) is a hot-rolled structural steel profile conforming to IS 808 specifications with slightly tapered inner flanges.

Does this calculation support tapered flanges?

This tool utilizes standard parallel-flange equivalent mean thickness (Tf) which matches standard commercial steel channel tables within ±1-2% accuracy.
