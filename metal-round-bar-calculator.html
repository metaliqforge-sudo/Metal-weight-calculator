How This Calculator Works

Theoretical solid round bar weight is derived directly from circular cross-sectional geometry:

Step 1 (Cross-Section Area): Area = π × (d / 2)² mm²
Step 2 (Volume cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Step 3 (Weight kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
Quick Steel Standard Rule: Weight (kg/m) ≈ d² ÷ 162.28 (where d is in mm)
