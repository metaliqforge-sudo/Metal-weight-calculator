Profile Definition:

How This Calculator Works

The cross-sectional area of a regular hexagon defined by distance across flats (s) is given by geometric trigonometry:

Cross-section Area: Area = (√3 / 2) × s² ≈ 0.866025 × s²
Volume (cm³): (Area mm² × Length mm) ÷ 1,000
Weight (kg): (Volume cm³ × Density g/cm³) ÷ 1,000
