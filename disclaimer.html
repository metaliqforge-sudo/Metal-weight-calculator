1. Theoretical & Estimative Nature of Calculations

While our algorithms use standard mathematical models (such as ISO/DIN pitch-line pitch cylinder calculations and standard geometric formulas), theoretical calculations cannot account for real-world manufacturing deviations.

2. Manufacturing Tolerances & Material Deviations

Actual physical fasteners differ slightly in weight from theoretical values due to:

  • Dimensional Tolerances: Standards (ISO 4014, ISO 4017, DIN 933, ASME B18.2.1) allow upper and lower limits on head height, width across flats, thread crest rounding, and shank diameter.
  • Material Density Variations: The density of commercial metal alloys (e.g., Mild Steel at 7.85 g/cm³, SS 304 at 8.00 g/cm³, Brass at 8.50 g/cm³) may vary marginally between batches, mill certificates, and temper grades.
  • Surface Coatings & Finishes: Electroplating, hot-dip galvanization, passivating, PTFE coating, or black oxide finishing alter the gross final weight.
