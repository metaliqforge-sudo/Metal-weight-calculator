Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. MetaliqForge values your privacy and handles all data transparently.

1. Information We Collect

MetaliqForge provides mathematical engineering calculators without requiring user registration or passwords. When you visit our website, standard web server logs and analytics may collect anonymous data including your browser type, device operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage (`localStorage`) to remember your unit preferences (Metric or Imperial) and your cookie consent status so you are not prompted repeatedly. We also partner with third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense which utilize cookies to serve ads based on prior visits.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to users based on visits to MetaliqForge and other sites across the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. Contact Inquiries

When you submit inquiries via our Contact Form, your name, email address, and message are transmitted securely to respond to your technical questions. We never sell, rent, or trade your email information to third parties.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at metaliqforge@gmail.com.
