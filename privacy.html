Privacy Policy

How MetaliqForge collects, protects, and handles information, along with our compliance regarding Google AdSense, cookies, and analytics.

Effective Date: October 2026

At MetaliqForge, accessible to global engineers, fabricators, and students, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by MetaliqForge and how we utilize it.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

1. Log Files

MetaliqForge follows standard procedures of using server log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose is to analyze trends, administer the site, track movement across pages, and gather demographic information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other modern web utility, MetaliqForge uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the specific pages on the website that the visitor accessed. This information helps us optimize the user experience and maintain fast calculation responsiveness.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve relevant advertisements to our visitors based on their visit to MetaliqForge and other sites across the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline or opt out of the use of DART cookies by reviewing the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Advertising Partners

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on MetaliqForge. These technologies measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and personalize the advertising content you see.

Please note that MetaliqForge has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

CCPA Privacy Rights

California consumers have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal data collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the sale of personal information. MetaliqForge does not sell any personal data.

GDPR Data Protection Rights

Every user is entitled to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of their personal data. If you submit a query via our contact form, you can request that we delete your communication history at any time.

5. Children's Information

MetaliqForge does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

Questions about our Privacy Policy? Feel free to contact us with any questions or requests.
