Privacy Policy

How MetaliqForge respects user privacy, handles client-side engineering calculations, and complies with international data standards.

1. Overview of Service & Client-Side Processing

MetaliqForge is an independent metallurgical engineering and industrial fastener weight calculation suite. All calculations performed across our online calculators (including Hex Bolts, Metal Pipes, Plates, Solid Bars, Structural Angles, Box Tubes, Channels, and Joists) are executed locally inside your web browser using JavaScript. Your dimension inputs, quantities, and calculated tonnages are never transmitted, stored, or indexed on our web servers.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, passwords, credit card information, or identity verification to use our calculation utilities. We collect minimal information under the following limited circumstances:

  • Contact Form Inquiries: If you voluntarily reach out via our contact page, your submitted name, email address, and message are utilized solely to respond to your technical feedback or engineering question.
  • Standard Server Logs: Like most web platforms, our hosting server logs basic HTTP requests (such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps) for security diagnostics and uptime monitoring.

3. Cookies & Advertising Practices

We use functional cookies to retain your preferred measurement system (Metric mm vs. Imperial inch) and remember cookie consent selections. Third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on prior visits to our site or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or using the universal cookie consent banner provided on our site.

4. Third-Party Links & External Resources

Our platform may link to international engineering references (such as ISO, DIN, ASTM, and ASME standards). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external websites.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in calculation algorithms or legal regulatory compliance. Changes will take effect immediately upon publication on this page.
